AI for Science

Lei Wei 卫雷

The work focuses on AI for Science: DNA language models, deep learning genomic prediction, and multi-omics foundation models. This includes architecture design for models that take genome, transcriptome, epigenome, and image data, plus machine-learning interpretability, with applications in biological breeding and medicine. The research has contributed to the National Science and Technology Major Project on Biological Breeding, the National Key R&D Program, and related programs. More than ten SCI papers have been published, and more than ten national invention patents and software copyrights have been filed. Genomic prediction, intelligent image analysis, and multimodal breeding models developed in this work have been deployed at listed agricultural companies, supporting intelligent breeding and precision medicine. 主要从事 AI for Science、DNA 大语言模型、深度学习基因组预测、多组学大模型等方向研究，重点围绕基因组、转录组、表观组、图像等多组学开展语言模型的架构设计、开发与机器学习可解释性方法研发，并探索人工智能大模型在生物育种与医学领域的应用。参与生物育种国家科技重大专项、国家重点研发计划、“十四五”重点研发专项等科研项目。已在 SCI 期刊发表论文十余篇，申请国家发明专利及软件著作权十余项。负责研发的基因组预测、图像智能分析及多模态一体化育种模型已在多家农业上市企业落地应用，为智能育种与精准医学提供技术支撑。

I. Research Interest I. 研究方向

01

Genomic and variant foundation models 基因组大模型，变异数据大模型

Language-model architectures for DNA and variant data, aimed at long-context genomic understanding and downstream prediction. 面向 DNA 与变异数据的语言模型架构，服务长上下文基因组理解与下游预测。

02

Deep learning phenotype prediction and multi-omics models 深度学习表型预测与多组学模型

Deep models for phenotype prediction, and multimodal systems that combine genomics, transcriptomics, and epigenomics. 深度学习表型预测，以及基因组学、转录组学、表观组学多模态模型开发。

03

Multi-omics breeding, medical models, and interpretability 多组学育种、医学大模型与可解释性

Applications in biological breeding and medicine, with model interpretability that can move from prediction to locus-level explanation. 多组学育种与医学大模型，以及从预测走到位点解释的模型可解释性方法。

V. Industry Contributions V. 行业贡献

The work covers genomic prediction, intelligent image analysis, and an integrated breeding model, including genomic selection, image segmentation, phenotype prediction, and breeding software. The pipeline runs from data collection and analysis to breeding decision support, and has been deployed at listed agricultural companies including Wen's Group and Sunner Group. It is used in livestock phenotyping, genetic evaluation, and precision selection, to raise the efficiency of breeding data processing and intelligent decision-making. 负责研发的基因组预测、图像智能分析与一体化智能育种模型，涵盖基因组选择、图像分割、表型预测、育种软件平台开发等。相关技术已形成从数据采集、智能分析到育种决策支持的完整技术链，并在温氏集团、圣农集团等农业上市企业实现落地应用，服务于畜禽表型测定、遗传评估与精准选育等实际生产环节，提升了育种数据处理效率和智能化决策水平，推动人工智能技术在生物育种中的产业化应用。
