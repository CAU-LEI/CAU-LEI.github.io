AI for Science

Lei Wei

The work focuses on AI for Science: DNA language models, deep learning genomic prediction, and multi-omics foundation models. This includes architecture design for models that take genome, transcriptome, epigenome, and image data, plus machine-learning interpretability, with applications in biological breeding and medicine. More than ten SCI papers have been published, with several more under review, and more than ten national invention patents and software copyrights have been filed. Genomic prediction, intelligent image analysis, and multimodal breeding models developed in this work have been deployed at listed agricultural companies, supporting intelligent breeding and precision medicine.

IV. Industry Contributions

The work covers genomic prediction, intelligent image analysis, and an integrated breeding model, including genomic selection, image segmentation, phenotype prediction, and breeding software. The pipeline runs from data collection and analysis to breeding decision support, and has been deployed at listed agricultural companies including Wen's Group and Sunner Group. It is used in livestock phenotyping, genetic evaluation, and precision selection, to raise the efficiency of breeding data processing and intelligent decision-making.
