Research研究

01

Interpretable genomic prediction 可解释基因组预测

AIGP is an AI breeding pipeline for feature selection, model building, acceleration, hyperparameter search, covariate handling, and SNP interpretability with SHAP. It covers regression and classification on genomic, transcriptomic, and related tabular omics data. AIGP 是一套人工智能育种流程，覆盖特征选择、建模、加速、参数搜索、协变量处理，以及基于 SHAP 的 SNP 可解释分析。它同时支持回归与分类，可用于基因组、转录组及相关组学表格数据。

02

Intelligent livestock phenotyping 畜禽智能表型

Deep segmentation and prediction for animal image and CT phenotypes, including Chicken-Mamba for testis analysis and a subcutaneous-fat pipeline from image enhancement to segmentation to trait prediction. 面向畜禽图像与 CT 表型的深度分割与预测，包括用于睾丸分析的 Chicken-Mamba，以及皮下脂肪从图像增强、分割到性状预测的完整流程。

03

Sequence models for genotype data 基因型序列模型

Transformer-style models for genotype sequences, plus conversational tooling that turns AIGP into a checkable analysis skill: inspect inputs, choose a defensible validation design, run models, and explain outputs. 面向基因型序列的 Transformer 类模型，以及把 AIGP 做成可核查分析技能的对话工具：检查输入、确定验证设计、运行模型并解释结果。
